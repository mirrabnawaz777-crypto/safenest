Common questions

How soon does my cover start?

Most policies start the day after you pay your first month. Health cover has a 30-day waiting period for illnesses you did not have before.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. You can pay monthly, every three months or once a year. Paying yearly saves you about 8%.

What if I want to cancel?

You can cancel at any time. Cancel within 14 days of buying and you get a full refund.

Does a claim make my price go up?

Not for health and life policies. For car and home, we only raise the price if you make more than one claim in a year.
